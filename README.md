This website is a technological adaptation of the classic fortune telling ball. The lines have been created from zero, inspired on the kind of sentences that it used to have.
You enter your name, so whan you get the awnser it feels more personal. 
Then you click the button that says "tell me my fourtune", and then it gives you your random fortune.
There is also an option to change the design of the ball color, the number, ...
